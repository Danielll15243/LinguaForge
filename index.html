Built to keep you going

A clear path

Each lesson must reach 75% to unlock the next. Your best score is kept, so you can retry without losing it.

Spaced repetition

Words move through review boxes with growing intervals of 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days, so you see them just before you would forget them.

Daily momentum

Points, a day streak and a daily goal you set yourself help you build a habit without pressure.

Czech or English

Switch the interface and explanations between Czech and English at any time. The language you learn stays German.

Light and dark

Follow your device, or pick a light or dark theme. The layout works on phone, tablet and desktop.

Private by design

Your progress is saved in your browser on your own device. You can export it as a file and import it again.

Questions

Which level does the course cover?

German from complete beginner (A0) to A1, organised in 14 units. Some units currently contain fewer exercises than others, and the content is still being expanded.

Do I need an account?

No. You can try the course as a guest. An account keeps the progress of different people on the same device apart, and is needed to activate a paid plan.

Where is my progress stored?

In your web browser, on your own device. It is not sent to our servers. Clearing your browser data removes it, so export a backup file from the app now and then. Progress is not synchronised between devices.

How do I activate a paid plan?

After you pay, you receive a licence code tied to your account name. Enter it on the Plans screen in the app. A Monthly licence is valid for 31 days and is renewed with a new code; a Lifetime licence has no expiry date.

Does audio and speaking practice work on every device?

Listening uses the German voice installed on your device. If there is none, the app shows the text instead. Speaking practice is self-checked: you repeat the phrases aloud, and the app does not do speech recognition.
