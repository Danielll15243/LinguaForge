2. In short

The LinguaForge app does not send your learning progress to us. It is saved in your web browser on your own device. We only receive personal data when you pay, when you write to us, and (as technical request data) when you visit this website.

4. Cookies and local storage

This website does not set cookies of its own. The app uses your browser’s local storage, and a session flag for guest mode, because it needs them to work. You can remove this data by deleting the account in the app, by using the app’s reset, or by clearing your browser data. Stripe may use its own cookies on its checkout pages; see Stripe’s privacy policy.

5. Legal bases

Where the EU or UK GDPR applies, we process personal data to perform our contract with you (payment and licence delivery), for our legitimate interests (operating and securing the website, answering your messages) and to meet legal obligations such as accounting.

6. Who receives data

Stripe (payments), Vercel (website hosting) and Google (fonts). These providers operate internationally, so data may be processed outside your country. We do not run advertising or analytics trackers on this website.

7. How long we keep data

We keep payment and accounting records for as long as accounting and legal obligations require, and messages for as long as needed to answer and follow up. Data stored in your browser stays there until you delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to receive it in a portable format, and to withdraw consent you have given. Write to daniel.martinovsky@seznam.cz. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. We cannot access, change or delete data that exists only on your device; you control it in your browser.

9. Children

The service is intended for people aged 13 or older.

10. Security

Local account passwords are stored only as salted hashes, and payments are handled by Stripe. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
